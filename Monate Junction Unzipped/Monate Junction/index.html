About-Us

Our Story

Monate Junction was born from one simple idea - everyone deserves food that is fast, fresh, and full of flavour without breaking the bank. We're not just another fast food joint. We're the corner where friends meet after work, where taxi drivers grab a quick bite, where families come for that Sunday treat. From juicy burgers to loaded fries and wings with a kick, everything we serve is made with real ingredients and that extra monate touch. We started small, with a grill, a big dream, and a love for good food. Today, that dream is your favourite junction.
